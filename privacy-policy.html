LandLedger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28-07-2026

LandLedger is a personal land-lease tracking app. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

What data the app stores

LandLedger stores the information you enter yourself: landowner names, lease locations, land area, lease dates, and payment records. This data is stored locally on your device in a private database that only the app can access.

Google Account access

If you choose to sign in with Google, LandLedger requests permission to create, read, and update files in a private, hidden storage area of your Google Drive account called the "application data folder." This area is:

  • Not visible in your normal Google Drive file list
  • Only accessible by this app — not by other apps or by Google Drive's web/mobile interface
  • Used solely to store one backup file containing the data described above, so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or use it on another device

LandLedger does not access, read, or modify any other files, folders, or data in your Google Drive. LandLedger does not access your Gmail messages, contacts, calendar, or any other Google service or data.

Data sharing

LandLedger does not sell, share, transmit, or provide your data to any third party, advertiser, or analytics service. The only place your data is stored is (a) locally on your device, and (b) if you sign in with Google, in the private Drive backup file described above, under your own Google account.

Data deletion

You can delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the app. You can delete the Drive backup at any time by revoking LandLedger's access from your Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which also deletes the associated application data folder.
