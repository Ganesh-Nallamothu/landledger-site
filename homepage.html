LandLedger

LandLedger is a mobile application for tracking leased agricultural and commercial land. It helps landowners and tenants keep an organized record of lease agreements, land area, and payments in one place.

Purpose of this application

LandLedger's purpose is to replace paper records and scattered notes with a single, simple app for managing land lease information. Specifically, it lets a user:

  • Record each leased land parcel: the landowner's name, the location, the total area leased, and the lease start/end dates
  • Track multiple payments made to a landowner over the life of a lease, with a running total, since land payments are often made in installments rather than a single lump sum
  • Optionally back up this data to the user's own Google Drive (in a private, hidden application-data area), so the records survive a phone upgrade, reinstall, or accidental data loss

LandLedger is built for individuals managing a small number of leased land parcels — for example, a farmer leasing additional fields, or a family managing inherited land that is leased out to tenants.

Platforms

LandLedger is available as an Android app.
