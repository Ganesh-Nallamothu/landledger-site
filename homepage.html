LandLedger

A simple app for tracking leased land — parcels, lease dates, area, and payments to landowners.

What it does

  • Records each leased land parcel: landowner name, location, area, and lease start/end dates
  • Tracks multiple payments made to a landowner over time, with a running total
  • Optionally backs up your data to your own Google Drive, so it's safe if you switch devices or reinstall the app

Platforms

LandLedger is available as an Android app.
